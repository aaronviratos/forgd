# 04. User flows

## 1. First open to first log (target: under 3 minutes)
1. Legal gate (age 18+, terms, disclaimer) → units.
2. Intro: 6 screens (name, body, goal, target, readiness screen, summary).
3. Lands on **Home**: card at Rookie level 1 with starting stats of 1 (or higher after starting-stat questions).
4. Home shows "Set your starting stats: 3 quick screens" → 9 questions → stats jump to their starting levels (the first "wow").
5. User logs their first thing: **+ › Log weight** (free), or **+ › Log with AI** on the paid tier ("weighed 212, slept 7 hours" → review → save). Stats and XP move. First level-up usually happens on day 1.

## 2. The second intro ("Build your plan")
Shown the next time they open the app (and once a day until done or dismissed). Each step feeds the next:
1. **Body check:** measurements + progress photo → AI body assessment.
2. **Training program:** AI builder tailored to goal (fat loss: more cardio and conditioning; gain or show: hypertrophy), a template, or manual.
3. **Nutrition plan:** targets and a sample day from goal, body, program days, labs and restrictions.
4. **Supplement plan:** supplement ideas from diet gaps and labs.
5. **Bloodwork check-in:** plan the retest; AI says which markers to recheck to confirm the plan is working.

## 3. A normal day
1. Morning: open app → Home header shows goal progress and the AI line → tap Today row → Morning tab → weight, BP, sleep (or one Log with AI sentence).
2. Training: Today › Training › Start workout → ✓ each set (previous values pre-filled, rest timer runs) → records flash → workout saved.
3. Meals: + › Add food (My foods first) or Log with AI after each meal.
4. Evening: checklist shows what's left; supplements tab; done.
5. Card levels update; streak increments.

## 4. Weekly
- Check-in day (Sunday default): Progress › Check-in (summary auto-filled; add waist, photos, notes).
- Every 2–4 weeks: + › Add measurements (+ photo).
- Monday: Home AI line reflects last week.

## 5. Lab cycle
1. + › Add lab results → photo of report → review values → save.
2. Labs page shows the Biomarker snapshot → Food for your biomarkers / Supplement ideas.
3. Plan next draw; AI lists markers to retest.
4. Next draw: Home progress chips show "N biomarkers improved".

## 6. Changing the plan
- Tap any day in Plan › My program or This week → editor opens at that day.
- Or Rebuild with AI → preview → Use it and edit.

## 7. Asking for help
- Coach pill (top bar) or Home coach bar → ask anything.
- On any page: AI check → result → "Ask the coach a follow-up" (opens Coach with the question sent).
- From a stat: "Talk it through with the coach".

## 8. Safety paths
- Red-flag symptom logged → urgent alert with 911 guidance on Home and Today.
- BP at or above 180/120 → urgent alert; repeated readings over the user's limit → warn alert.
- Readiness screen "Yes" → doctor-clearance reminder in intro summary and in AI answers.
- Food relationship flagged → nutrition features switch to flexible, near-maintenance coaching.
