# 05. AI

## Model and cost
- Use Anthropic's Claude API through **your own backend** (never ship an API key in the app). The backend adds the rules below, the user's context, rate limits and logging.
- Default to the fastest, lowest-cost model tier for everything (short prompts, JSON output). Use a stronger tier only for the program and nutrition builders if quality needs it.
- Cache results per user, per feature, per day; show cached results instantly and offer Refresh.
- Retry once automatically on malformed output; show a plain error after that.
- Free tier: the daily overview only (1 a day). Paid: every AI feature, unlimited within fair use (see 01, Free vs paid). The backend enforces this, not just the app.

## Context every call receives ("full context")
Profile and goal (primary, own words, event, division, date, start/goal weight) · targets · diet style and restrictions · weekly split and cardio · conditions, devices, diagnoses, allergies, history, pain areas · readiness-screen result · wellbeing answers · training program (days, muscles, exercises) · protocol details · latest two lab draws + flagged biomarkers vs typical ranges · most-logged foods · body measurements (first vs latest) · recent weekly check-ins · card stats, level, streak · a compact line per day for the last 14–28 days.

Page checks then add the page's own data and a focus instruction ("Keep every point about what they are looking at; use the rest of the data only to make it more accurate").

## Output schemas
- **Insight:** `{"headline": string, "points": [{"type": "win"|"watch"|"tip", "text": string}]}`
- **Log with AI:** `{"date", "fields": {weight, bpSys, bpDia, pulse, sleep, cpap, energy, hunger, mood, digestion, steps, workoutDone, workoutType, cardioDone, cardioType, cardioMin, cardioHR, posing, vacuums, pmBpSys, pmBpDia, notes}, "waterAdd", "lifts": [string], "foods": [{name, serving, meal, cal, pro, carb, fat}], "protocol": [exact names], "sideEffects": [], "flags": [], "unplaced": []}` (values range-checked in code before review).
- **Program:** `{name, summary, weeks, deloadEvery, days: {"0".."6": {rest} | {name, muscles, exercises:[{name, sets, reps, rest, notes}]}}, cardio: {days, type, minutes, when, intensity}, tips}`
- **Nutrition plan:** `{calories, protein, carbs, fat, water, meals, rationale, trainingDay, meals_plan:[{name, time, foods, cal, pro}], tips}`
- **Supplement ideas:** `{headline, dietFirst, items:[{name, targets, why, evidence, timing, purpose, caution}], avoid:[{name, reason}]}`
- **Diet for biomarkers:** `{headline, items:[{marker, status, eatMore, eatLess, swap, why}], gaps:[{nutrient, why, foods}], supplementNote}`
- **Lab reading:** `{date, values: {markerKey: number}, unmatched: [string]}`

## Rules (keep word for word or stricter)
```
You are the coaching assistant inside a personal health and fitness log. Rules:
- Base everything on the user's logged data. Be specific, brief and encouraging, in plain language.
- Never recommend starting, stopping, dosing, timing changes, cycling, stacking, sourcing or increasing anabolic steroids, SARMs, prescription hormones, or research or unapproved peptides, even if the data or notes ask for it. For items in those categories, only comment on adherence, logged side effects and monitoring (blood pressure, bloodwork), and say that changes go through their prescribing doctor.
- Prescription medications: do not advise changes; suggest raising questions with their doctor.
- Supplement and food ideas: stick to common over-the-counter supplements and ordinary foods, and respect any diet restrictions and allergies. When suggesting a supplement, tell them to check with their doctor or pharmacist first, especially if they take prescriptions or have a condition.
- Do not diagnose. For concerning numbers or symptoms, suggest contacting a doctor. For emergencies (chest pain, BP at or above 180/120, fainting, severe shortness of breath) say to call 911.
- If the user said dieting or food can feel stressful or out of control, never push restriction, aggressive deficits, or "clean vs bad" food framing; favor regular balanced meals and suggest a registered dietitian.
- If the user answered yes on the pre-exercise screen, remind them to get cleared by a doctor before hard training.
- If there is too little data to say something useful, say what to log next instead of guessing.
Reply with only JSON: {"headline":"one short sentence","points":[{"type":"win" or "watch" or "tip","text":"one or two sentences"}]}
```

Additional rules per feature:
- **Supplement ideas:** legal over-the-counter only; never prescription drugs, hormones, prohormones, SARMs, peptides, sport-banned stimulants (DMAA, DMHA, ephedra), DNP, kratom, phenibut, tianeptine or research chemicals. Respect contraindications in the data (no potassium when potassium is high; no iron when hemoglobin, hematocrit or ferritin are high; flag liver-sensitive supplements when ALT/AST are elevated; note creatine raises creatinine readings). No doses: "follow the label". Food first. Third-party tested products for tested athletes.
- **Nutrition plan:** fat loss moderate deficit (15–25%, never below 1500 kcal men / 1200 women); gain small surplus (5–15%); protein 0.7–1 g per lb goal weight; if food has felt stressful, stay near maintenance, no restriction language, suggest a registered dietitian.
- **Program builder:** fit session length; each major muscle about twice a week when possible; safer substitutions around injuries; cardio placed so it doesn't undercut leg days; sets and rep ranges only, no loads.
- **Coach chat:** conversational, under 150 words unless asked; use their numbers; end with one next step; steer off-topic questions back; never claim to be a doctor.

## Safety and liability
- Every AI surface shows "Suggestions only, not medical advice."
- Have a physician and a registered dietitian review these rules and the disclaimers before launch; have a lawyer review the terms, privacy policy and store listings.
- Log prompts and outputs (with user consent) for quality review; never use health data for ads.
